Hostel & Transport Facilities

As students from outside of Siliguri also come to Sankalp to attend various courses, especially during the winter season, we have arranged completely safe and suitable hostels to accommodate them. Separate hostels for boys and girls are available, adjacent to the institute.

Hostel Guidelines

  • Students must behave decently and be respectful towards staff.
  • No electronic gadgets for personal use without permission.
  • Objectionable articles, smoking, etc., are strictly prohibited.
  • Students must be responsible for personal articles & damages.
  • Study time strictly abided by. Rooms kept neat and clean.
  • Outings are not allowed except with parents (on request).
