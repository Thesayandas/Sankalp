Student Facilities

Comprehensive support systems designed to help every student maximize their potential and achieve their goals.

Online Backup Classes

(Recorded Support)

  • All physical classroom lectures are captured in high-definition video.
  • Seamlessly access these recordings as your digital academic backup.
  • Videos remain active for 30 days, empowering you to:
    • Reinforce core fundamentals
    • Replay complex topics for better retention
    • Study and review independently at your preferred speed

Lifetime Access to Solved Modules

  • Comprehensive, step-by-step video solutions for the entire curriculum.

  • Enjoy unrestricted, lifetime availability to these resources to support your ongoing educational journey long after your batch ends.

  • Participate in real-time, in-depth breakdowns of every examination.

Short Tricks & Quick Concepts

Motivation & Career Guidance
